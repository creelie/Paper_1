Editors, Annals of Mathematics

Dear Editors,

Please consider "Unbounded strong-product ratios for the Grundy domination
number" for publication in the Annals of Mathematics.

The paper disproves the strong-product multiplicativity conjecture of Bresar,
Bujtas, Gologranc, Klavzar, Kosmrlj, Patkos, Tuza and Vizer in a quantitative
form: no constant multiple of the product of the two factor parameters is
an upper bound. Explicit graphs on five-element subsets have factor bounds
m and binom(m,2), while their product has a legal sequence of length binom(m,5).
The proof combines a triangular rank bound for exact neighbourhood support
with two finite-field incidence matrices. The underlying congruence method
is classical and is credited to Alon; the manuscript proves the application
in full rather than relying on an external assertion about Shannon capacity.

A separate elementary half-graph argument determines gamma_gr(H_N) for every
N >= 6 and yields a counterexample on 114 vertices with the octahedron as
the first factor. The main results do not depend on computer calculations.
For comparison, an appendix describes a Lean certificate for the earlier
N=21 example. Its scope is expressly distinguished from the two general proofs.

The original preprint is https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00494v1. Its original
computational development is archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23378021.
The submission revision and reproduction files are at
https://github.com/creelie/Paper_1. This revision has the preprint DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23607502.
The manuscript describes AI assistance and
identifies the affected proof sections in accordance with the journal's policy.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
