# Annals submission notes

Prepared 24 October 2026. The manuscript and letter are prepared for submission;
no journal submission has been made.

Submit the final PDF at https://ef.msp.org/submit/annals . The official instructions
are https://annals.math.princeton.edu/submission-guidelines (checked 24 October 2026).
The package uses aomart, a self-contained abstract below 200 words, BibTeX and vector
PDF diagrams. Its first page does not claim Annals publication, a journal DOI or
publisher copyright.

The journal requires a human author to take responsibility for correctness and
citations and to identify ideas contributed by AI and their locations. The
manuscript's disclosure identifies the exact-support/two-field argument and the
all-parameter half-graph argument by section. Review that account for completeness.

Before the actual journal submission, the author must supply truthful answers
about competing submissions, conflicts, funding and institutional details when
the portal asks. This package does not assert facts about those matters that were
not provided. Review the listed former affiliation and contact details.

The earlier DOI is a source-archive DOI, not the DOI of the present revision.
The current revision DOI must be confirmed before the requested final ZIP is made.

No acceptance probability is assigned. Annals is an ambitious editorial target:
the construction resolves a specialized graph-invariant conjecture by an elementary
adaptation of a classical method. Its broader significance is for editors and
referees to assess. The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics published the original
conjecture and is a directly relevant alternative if the Annals editors decline it.
Submit to one journal at a time.
