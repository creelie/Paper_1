## Disclosure and scope

The paper proves that strong-product ratios for the Grundy domination number are unbounded, using an explicit two-field incidence construction. It also determines an all-parameter graph family and supplies a 114-vertex counterexample to multiplicativity. The general proofs are elementary and independent of finite computation.

Formal-check scope: The Lean certificate verifies the original finite N=21 counterexample. The general unbounded-ratio theorem and all-parameter formula are written proofs, not a general Lean formalisation.
